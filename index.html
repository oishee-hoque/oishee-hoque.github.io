Graduating in 2027 and open to applied and research scientist roles.

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Virginia and a recipient of the UVA SEAS Endowed Fellowship, advised by Madhav Marathe at the Biocomplexity Institute. I build computer vision systems: multimodal and vision–language models that bring domain knowledge into detection, segmentation and scene understanding.

My PhD research is in computer vision for Earth observation: knowledge-guided and multimodal learning, vision–language models, and foundation-model adaptation for remote sensing, applied to agricultural and environmental monitoring. I design models that fold domain priors into segmentation and detection, and I build the large-scale, ML-ready geospatial datasets that make those models trainable and testable. Work from my PhD and internships has appeared at NeurIPS, KDD, IJCAI, WACV and CVPR workshops.

Core skills
Multimodal fusion Semantic and instance segmentation Object detection Geospatial AI Foundation-model adaptation Vision–language models ML-ready dataset pipelines

Research areas

  • Knowledge-guided multimodal models. Segmentation and detection models that take domain structure as input instead of relearning it: crop and land-use priors through cross-attention, graph constraints on canal networks, and geometric priors on infrastructure. KIIMIGraSSPRISM-CAFO
  • ML-ready datasets and benchmarks. Scalable pipelines that turn raw satellite and UAV archives into annotated, multimodal benchmarks with metadata, held-out sites and task-specific metrics. IrrMapIRRISIGHTCAFOSatCWDBench
  • Foundation models and agentic geospatial systems. Adapting SAM-, DINO- and Swin-based models and vision–language models to remote sensing, and composing LLM agents with deterministic geospatial tools for operational decision support. ARFACWDBench
