Core skills

Multimodal fusion Semantic and instance segmentation Object detection Geospatial AI Foundation-model adaptation Vision–language models ML-ready dataset pipelines

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Virginia and a recipient of the UVA SEAS Endowed Fellowship, advised by Madhav Marathe at the Biocomplexity Institute. I build computer vision systems: multimodal and vision–language models that bring domain knowledge into detection, segmentation and scene understanding.

My PhD research applies this to Earth observation, for agricultural and environmental monitoring, in three threads:

Knowledge-guided multimodal models

Segmentation and detection models that take domain structure as input instead of relearning it: crop and land-use priors through cross-attention, graph constraints on canal networks, and geometric priors on infrastructure.

KIIM IGraSS PRISM-CAFO

ML-ready datasets and benchmarks

Scalable pipelines that turn raw satellite and UAV archives into annotated, multimodal benchmarks with metadata, held-out sites and task-specific metrics.

IrrMap IRRISIGHT CAFOSat CWDBench

Foundation models and agentic geospatial systems

Adapting SAM-, DINO- and Swin-based models and vision–language models to remote sensing, and composing LLM agents with deterministic geospatial tools for operational decision support.

ARFA CWDBench

Work from my PhD and internships has appeared at NeurIPS, KDD, IJCAI, WACV and CVPR workshops.

Graduating in 2027 and open to applied and research scientist roles.
